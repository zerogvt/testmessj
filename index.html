testmess

One multiple-choice test in Word, any number of shuffled variants — each written twice: a student copy (questions and options) and a professor copy (the same paper, plus the answer key on its own last page).

Your test never leaves this tab. There is no upload and no server: the documents are read, shuffled and written here, on your computer.

2 Settings

The same seed always produces exactly the same papers, which is what you want if you have to reprint one of them later. The seed used is shown with the results.

3 Build the papers

Equations written with the Word equation editor survive untouched: every stem and option is carried as the raw paragraph markup from your document, and the only thing rewritten is the label in front of it.
